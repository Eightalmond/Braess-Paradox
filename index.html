The network

    Congestible edges get slower as more drivers use them; the fixed edges cost 45 no matter what. The dashed A→B shortcut is free (cost 0).

    How to read it

    1. Press Play. Drivers spread out until the two routes tie at 65 — that's the equilibrium.
    2. Press Add shortcut. The free road looks like a bargain, so everyone takes it.
    3. Watch the chart: average travel time settles at 80. A free road made every single driver worse off.
    4. Remove the shortcut and the system relaxes back to 65.
